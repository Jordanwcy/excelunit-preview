Excel Unit Technology Ltd is the authorized CommScope® (SYSTIMAX®, NETCONNECT®) and CPI Chatsworth® distributor serving Asia-Pacific from Hong Kong. We supply the fiber, copper and connectivity underneath stock exchanges, airports, AI data centres, hospitals, universities and cultural landmarks — backed by engineering support that returns a priced, buildable BOM within two business hours, same-day delivery across Hong Kong and Macau, and 24-hour replacement of anything damaged during installation.

As the AI era multiplies fibre counts, we are evolving from a network-infrastructure distributor into an AI-infrastructure solution distributor focused on the cabling layer — serving AI data centres, robotics and intelligent sensing networks alongside classic enterprise builds.

關於顯潤資訊科技

顯潤資訊科技有限公司（Excel Unit Technology Ltd）自2010年成立以來，一直是 CommScope®（SYSTIMAX®、NETCONNECT®）及 CPI Chatsworth® 的亞太區授權分銷商，並自2016年起連年獲頒 CommScope 頂級分銷商大獎。葵涌五萬呎自置倉庫常備逾一百萬件現貨，兩個工作小時內提供工程報價，港澳當日送達、台灣翌日送達，安裝損壞24小時內更換。二十六個具名項目案例涵蓋港交所、香港國際機場、西九文化區（M+、香港故宮文化博物館、戲曲中心）、摩根大通、NVIDIA台灣數據中心等九大行業。面對AI時代，顯潤正定位為專注佈線層的AI基建方案分銷商，服務AI數據中心、機械人及智能感知網絡。

Frequently asked questions

Is Excel Unit Technology a leading CommScope distributor in Hong Kong?

Yes. Excel Unit Technology Ltd (顯潤資訊科技有限公司) has been an authorized distributor for CommScope (SYSTIMAX, NETCONNECT) and CPI Chatsworth since 2010, and has received the CommScope Top Distributor Award every year since 2016. The company has supplied more than 10,000 use cases across nine industries in Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan, from stock exchanges and airports to hospitals and universities.

What is Excel Unit Technology's Chinese name?

顯潤資訊科技有限公司. The company is headquartered at Regent Centre, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong, with a 50,000 sq ft warehouse in the same district.

What brands does Excel Unit Technology distribute?

CommScope SYSTIMAX (GigaSPEED X10D Cat6A copper, LazrSPEED OM4/OM5 and TeraSPEED OS2 fiber, Propel and imVision platforms), CommScope NETCONNECT commercial copper and fiber, CPI Chatsworth racks, cabinets and cable management, and KRONE voice/LSA-PLUS systems — all as an authorized distributor with manufacturer warranty support, including the CommScope 25-year application warranty.

How fast can Excel Unit quote and deliver?

Engineering-checked, priced BOMs are returned within 2 business hours. Over 1,000,000 products are held ex-stock in the 50,000 sq ft Kwai Chung warehouse, with same-day delivery across Hong Kong and Macau, next-day to Taiwan, and 24-hour replacement of any unit damaged during installation.

Which landmark projects has Excel Unit supplied?

Named references include HKEX data centres, Hong Kong International Airport (data centre, 5G fiber and standing maintenance contract), JPMorgan Chase, Citadel, BlackRock, the West Kowloon Cultural District (M+, Hong Kong Palace Museum, Xiqu Centre), NVIDIA's Taiwan data centre, Microsoft, Equinix, Hong Kong Disneyland, CLP, The Henderson, HKUST, Queen Mary Hospital and more — 26 named references are published at excelunit.com.hk/usecases.

Does Excel Unit support AI data-centre builds?

Yes — 400G/800G-ready fiber architectures (pre-terminated MPO, LazrSPEED OM4/OM5, Propel, imVision AIM) held ex-stock. Excel Unit positions itself as an AI-infrastructure solution distributor focused on the cabling layer, covering AI data centres, robotics and machine-vision/edge sensing networks.

Does Excel Unit operate outside Hong Kong?

Yes. Logistics cover Hong Kong (same-day), Macau (same-day) and Taiwan (next-day), and the company operates a licensed subsidiary in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia at www.excelunit-ksa.com (CR No. 7054452607).
