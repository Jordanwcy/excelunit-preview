Business Consulting & Strategy

Excel backs every project with deep CommScope SYSTIMAX, NETCONNECT and CPI product knowledge. Diverse Stockholding

Our 50,000 sq ft purpose-built warehouse in Kwai Chung holds over 1,000,000 products ex-stock across SYSTIMAX, NETCONNECT and CPI — one of the deepest structured-cabling inventories in Hong Kong. For live projects we ring-fence stock against your project ID so multi-month rollouts never stall.

About Excel Unit 關於顯潤

Excel Unit Technology Limited (顯潤資訊科技有限公司) has been the authorized CommScope and CPI Chatsworth distributor for Hong Kong and Macau for more than twenty years. We hold deep local stock of structured cabling, fiber optics and data-center infrastructure — so system integrators, contractors and IT teams get genuine product, distributor pricing, and same-week delivery.
